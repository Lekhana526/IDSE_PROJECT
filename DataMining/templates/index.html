DATA MINER

THE TREASURE HUNT

An Interactive 2D Adventure Game for Learning Data Science with Python

HOW TO PLAY

KEYBOARD CONTROLS

• WASD / Arrow Keys: Move your miner through underground caves.

• Spacebar: Swing pickaxe to mine rocks & open unlocked chests.

• E Key: Activate handheld Data Scanner or connect to terminals.

• Esc Key: Pause the game.

MINING & CHARGING SURVIVAL

• Mine breakable rocks (Ruby, Emerald, Gold, Diamond) to earn points and gems.

• Keep an eye on your Charging Level! Traversing caves and mining uses battery power.

• Touch glowing ⚡ Charging Capsules scattered throughout the caverns to restore +3% battery charge (capsules disappear once collected and cannot be reused; cleanly capped at 100%)!

DATA SCIENCE MISSIONS & PROGRESSION

• Each cave level contains an ancient Data Terminal with data science challenges.

• Stand near the terminal and press [E] to launch the Data Scanner.

• Complete the mission task to unlock the Level's Treasure Chest.

• Open the chest to unlock the mystical Gateway to advance to the next level!

• Progress sequentially through all 5 cave levels to reach the Mountain King's Golden Vault!
